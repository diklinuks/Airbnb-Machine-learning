The question

Can the listing itself tell you what a night should cost?

I started out trying to predict nightly prices. The best model topped out at R² ≈ 0.53 — useful, but not enough to give a real number to a real host. Static facts about a listing don't capture what really moves price: season, events, demand. So I changed the question.

Instead of guessing a price, I asked what kind of listing is this? — and the answer turned out to be cleaner, more honest, and more interesting to look at.

Behind the scenes

How this was built.

1 · Cleaned the data

Inside Airbnb's March 2025 snapshot, joined with the booking calendar for a real price signal, with bathrooms parsed into number + kind and missing beds imputed with a Random Forest.

2 · Tried to predict price

Ridge, Random Forest and XGBoost on log-price. Best was XGBoost at R² ≈ 0.53. SHAP and partial-dependence confirmed the static features hit a ceiling.

3 · Pivoted to segmentation

Stratified k-means on structural features only — price is never an input. The five groups you're looking at fall out, and they're stable across the k-selection sweep.

4 · Shipped it

This page. Vanilla JS, Leaflet for the map, Chart.js for the comparisons, nothing to install. The whole dataset is one 15 KB JSON.
